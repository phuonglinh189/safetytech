Construction Safety Monitoring and Inspection Technologies

📋 Assessment Instructions

What is this assessment?

This is a Digital Technology Adoption Maturity Assessment that evaluates your organization's readiness to implement and use digital safety technologies in construction monitoring and inspection. It covers 22 key indicators across 4 categories: Strategy, Operations, People, and Technology.

How to rank each indicator?

For each of the 22 indicators, select the level (1-5) that best describes your organization's current state:

  • Level 1: Initial (Ad-hoc, unpredictable)
  • Level 2: Managed (Basic processes with controls)
  • Level 3: Defined (Standardized, documented)
  • Level 4: Quantitatively Managed (Measured and controlled)
  • Level 5: Optimizing (Continuous improvement)

What will you get?

After completing all 22 indicators and clicking "Calculate", you'll receive:

  • ✓ Overall maturity score (1-5 scale)
  • ✓ Category breakdown scores (Strategy, Operations, People, Technology)
  • ✓ Visual charts and performance distribution
  • ✓ Detailed assessment report (exportable as PDF)

What do the results mean?

Your results show where your organization stands in adopting digital safety technologies. A higher score (closer to 5) indicates more mature processes, better documentation, measurement, and continuous improvement. Use these results to identify improvement areas and guide your digital transformation strategy.
